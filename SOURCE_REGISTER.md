# Source register and mapping limitations

- **Original official questionnaire**: `../Supplier Questionnaire Survey and Rating.pdf`, 30 pages, 2025 Microsoft Forms. Q1–Q6 are supplier identification; Q7–Q61 are 55 1–5 rating questions grouped in seven survey categories. Prompts and approximate original page numbers are transcribed in `official_questionnaire.json`, but full item-specific response anchors remain in the PDF. Do not claim the 55-item survey maps one-to-one to A1–C4.
- **Team research criteria**: `criteria.csv`, 16 project criteria (A1–A5 Future Business Readiness, B1–B7 Production-System Capability, C1–C4 Resilience / Continuity).
- **Illustrative crosswalk**: Each official rated question has a `illustrativeCriterion` field, drawn up for prototype testing. A low answer only flags an item for assessor investigation, not a verified supplier gap or Siemens-approved weighted score.
- **Four benchmark research documents**: `benchmark_research.json`, extracted *without semantic rewriting* from the user-supplied `SE-Capability-Studio (1).html` embedded Google Docs data, with company chapters, paragraph indices and source URLs. Full paragraph search operates locally against this source.
- **Intervention catalog**: `interventions.json`, 12 existing working case records plus 9 supplementary candidates from the October 29 deck and sponsor discussion, attributed to named slides in the `reference` field. `KEEP` means a research-based scoped intervention, not an approved Siemens recommendation; `INVESTIGATE` means evidence or applicability is incomplete.
- **Dummy Snowflake views**: `snowflake_mock.json`, supplier risk, performance, evaluation and relationship management. All four supplier profiles are fictional, and this is *not* a Snowflake export.
- **Dummy case files**: `cases_seed.json`, four fictitious scenarios, with two-sided financial assumptions, pre-award uncertainties, action records, example reviews, and draft survey answers. The completed Atlas case is a simulated training example rather than an actual achieved result.
- **November 5 sponsor discussion**: User-provided transcript and summary underpin the proposed separation between Siemens benefit and supplier incentive, risk of pre-award CapEx/OpEx, productivity-ask relief, capacity flexibility, and Siemens IT's likely Snowflake + Microsoft ecosystem.

**Model boundaries**: The scoring logic, financial uncertainty assumptions, interventions, survey crosswalk, modeled P(success), and all approvals require sponsor and expert validation. A financial engine is not a sourcing award decision engine.
